ARCHITECTURE EVIDENCE

Designed to be reviewed, challenged, and operated.

01Requirements & quality attributes

Each case study starts with hard constraints and ties availability, security, privacy, consistency, cost, recovery, and maintainability back to an explicit design response.

02Decisions & failure modes

ADRs explain why services were selected or omitted, while failure-path diagrams show what degrades, what fails over, and where the architecture deliberately fails closed.

03Validation without overclaiming

Runtime evidence is recorded only where it has actually been exercised: the portfolio hosting stack is live, while the e-commerce and age-assurance projects continue to separate compiler/control-plane validation from future runtime proof.

BUSINESS & STAKEHOLDER IMPACT

Architecture measured by what changes for the business.

Selected internal initiatives where architecture and automation changed service delivery, identity governance, endpoint risk, or recovery operations—not just the underlying technology.

Operational outcomes
Service delivery
87–92%faster provisioning

Provisioning automation

Connected Freshservice, Entra ID, and Intune to reduce deployment from 2.5–3 hours to 15–20 minutes, cutting hands-on fulfillment time and accelerating endpoint readiness.

Identity governance
~100stale accounts discovered

Continuous account lifecycle management

Identified dormant identities left behind by a manual-only offboarding process, then designed a weekly control that detects inactivity, disables accounts, moves them into a holding group, and removes them after a defined retention period.

CIO-facing proposal
Risk reduction
Least privilegeinstead of standing local admin

Endpoint privilege modernization

Replaced broad local administrator rights with policy-based Endpoint Privilege Management, reducing the potential endpoint blast radius while preserving approved elevation paths for users and support teams.

CIO-facing proposal
Resilience
~15 mininitial travel removed from the recovery path

Remote warehouse recovery

Used EPM-enabled remote access to remove a warehouse drive and per-device movement from the critical recovery path. Problem resolution stays the same; recovery can begin sooner and scales better when multiple endpoints are affected.

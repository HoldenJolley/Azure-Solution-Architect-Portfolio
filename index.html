ARCHITECTURE EVIDENCE

Designed to be reviewed, challenged, and operated.

01Requirements & quality attributes

Each case study starts with hard constraints and ties availability, security, privacy, consistency, cost, recovery, and maintainability back to an explicit design response.

02Decisions & failure modes

ADRs explain why services were selected or omitted, while failure-path diagrams show what degrades, what fails over, and where the architecture deliberately fails closed.

03Validation without overclaiming

Bicep compiler checks and live Azure ARM What-If are separated from runtime proof. Every project states what is validated today and what still requires deployment and testing.
